TW̌I MMEBUSE̲M.
B.
1. E̲ba a, e̲kã onī.
2. “E̲ba a, mebo̲ āno [:*miso āno, misom’]“ nè “E̲tõtõ a,
mesaṅ“, – woṅ mu hena akyi [:afã] na wowo̲? – Mewo̲
“E̲ba a, mebo̲ āno“ akyi.
3. “E̲reba, e̲reba“ na e̲ye̲ hũ; na enyã ba a, e̲nyé̲ hũ bio.
4. “Mereba, mereye̲ ansã“ na e̲mãã o̲kwakuo dua kaa ho̲.
5. Wommá nte̲m a, womã oguaṅ.
6. O̲ba a o̲be̲ye̲ yiye, wonnyá no [:wo̲nyé̲ṅ no] ke̲te̲-pa
so ṅkõ.
7. O̲ba se̲ o̲se, na[nso] o̲wo̲ abusũa [:mmuṡũa].
8. O̲ba nsũ a, wo̲mmá no nũfu?
9. Wo ba ko̲ sumănã so na o̲wo̲ ka no a, wuntw̌a ho̲
ṅkyene, na woye̲ no [:ho̲] aduru.
10. Wo ba nẽ to [:gu] wo sĕre̲ so a, wode baha [:mposae]
na eyi [:e̲popa], na womfá o̲sekaṅ ntw̌a [:na wuntw̌á
ṅkyene]. Cf. 569.
11. Wo ba saw asa-bone a, se no se̲: wo asaw nye̲ fe̲;
na nse no se̲: o̲kra tete gu mu. B.
12. Wo ba sisi wo kora ba a, enyé; nanso wo kora ba
sisi wo ba a, enye. R. 262.
13. O̲ba-bone nnim kasakyere̲.
* The words in brackets with two dots before them may be
put instead of the words preceding them; those without the two
dots may be added or left out.
